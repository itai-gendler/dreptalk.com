# Visitor

A person reading DRepTalk without signing in. Reading is public: governance actions, threads, DRep profiles, analytics, the treasury and the Governance Review need no account. A visitor signs in with a Cardano key to take part, and may register as a DRep or delegate from a wallet without signing in.
