# Governance Review edition

A written account of Cardano governance over a window of epochs: what was decided, what moved and what is still open, with its sources and the saved dataset it was written from.

## Information kept

- **Epoch window** — the epochs it covers
- **Title** — its headline
- **Article** — the written edition with its charts
- **Sources** — everything it took from proposal documents, rationales and other pages
- **Dataset** — the saved dataset it was written from
- **Corrections** — dated corrections listed at the top
- **Announced** — whether accounts were told about it
